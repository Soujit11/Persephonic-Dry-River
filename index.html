Dry River

VR build · 15 Nov

A dry riverbed under the summer sun, and a ruined stone house on its bank. Touch the riverbed and the water flows again. Keep your hands on the ruins and the house slowly rebuilds itself. On a screen, click the riverbed, hover over the house to rebuild it, drag to look, and walk with W A S D. When the house stands again, light the lantern and open the door. Inside, everything answers a touch: the fire and the wood basket, the candles, the clock, the kettle (when it whistles, lift it off the fire and tip it over the cup), the sofa and the armchair, the cat, the books, the curtains, the lamps, the plant, the cloth for the dirty windows, the fish and the gramophone. Wind chimes hang by the door outside. In VR, use your hands or the controllers: point at the dry riverbed and pinch (or press the trigger) to call the water back. Hold the pinch or trigger on the bank to water the meadow, or on the ruins to rebuild the house; press briefly to step there. Press A or X to put down what you hold. Your right hand does the touching: reach for something and pinch or press the trigger to light, open, pick up or sit. The left hand only walks. With controllers, the left thumbstick walks and the right one turns. Turn on Echo before you enter VR and speak: the hills call your voice back.
